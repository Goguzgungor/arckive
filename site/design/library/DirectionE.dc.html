Even if the network blips, no data is lost.

A poll-based backbone chosen for loss-free, self-healing ingestion — not just latency.

  • Cursor + checkpoint — the last processed block lives in Postgres; after any crash it resumes exactly there.
  • Gap-free backfill — event inserts and cursor advance share one transaction. A commit, or nothing.
  • Reorg-safe on Arc — indexes to the finalized tag; BFT finality means finalized blocks never reorg.
  • RPC failover — a health-checked pool with rotation, backoff, circuit-breaker. A blip never becomes a gap.
